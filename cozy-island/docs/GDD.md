# Cozy Island — Game Design Document

## Vision

Wake on a gentle tropical shore, explore at your own pace, experiment with found materials to unlock recipes, and turn your camp from a bare shelter into a self-sufficient cozy homestead.

## Core Loops

1. **Explore** — walk the island, discover zones, gather resources
2. **Survive (soft)** — manage hunger, thirst, energy; rest and cook at camp
3. **Craft & discover** — combine 3 items + tool; unlock recipe book entries
4. **Automate** — research workshop, assign helpers, unlock production chains

## Zones

| Zone | Resources | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Beach | sticks, berries, vine | Starting area |
| Forest | logs (axe), stone, flint, berries | Mid-game tools needed |
| River | fish, water, clay, iron ore | Fishing and smelting |
| Camp Cave | stone, vine, buildings | Home base, sleep, NPC Mira |

## Progression

1. Shipwreck on beach with starter items
2. Craft knife → axe → expand gathering
3. Build fire pit, bed, storage at camp
4. Research workshop → assign helpers
5. Unlock raft blueprint (MVP win state)

## Cozy Design Principles

- Meters decay slowly; zero applies debuffs, not death
- Relaxed Mode halves decay rate
- Failed crafts may lose materials but never hard-lock progress
- Warm palette, day/night ambient tint, companion dialogue hints

## Reference Mix

- **Lost in Blue** — island routine, camp life, exploration mystery
- **Dawn of Crafting** — recipe experimentation, skills, unfolding mechanics
- **Armory & Machine** — research tree, helper jobs, resource balance

## Post-MVP Ideas

- Multiple endings (escape vs stay)
- Domestic animals, traps, seasonal events
- Procedural island expansion
- Pixel art asset pass and ambient audio
